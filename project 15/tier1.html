The BMI formula

Metric: BMI = weight(kg) ÷ height(m)²

Height in cm must be converted to m first: cm ÷ 100

Imperial:BMI = (weight(lbs) ÷ height(in)²) × 703

Math.pow(height, 2) — raises height to the power of 2 (squares it)
